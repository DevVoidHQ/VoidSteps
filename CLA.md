# Contributor Licence Agreement

## Why this exists

VoidSteps is GPL-3.0-or-later. Without an agreement like this one, every
contributor holds copyright in their own contribution, and the project can never
change its licence again without tracking down all of them and getting each to
agree.

That is not theoretical here. The licence has already moved once, from MIT to
GPL-3.0, when the reasoning behind the original choice stopped being true.

**You keep your copyright.** This is a licence grant, not an assignment. You are
not signing your work over. You are giving the project permission to use and
relicense it, and you keep every right to your own contribution, including using
it elsewhere however you like.

## How to agree

The first time you open a pull request, paste this into a comment on it:

> I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.

That covers every contribution you make to this project, then and later. You only
do it once.

A maintainer then records it in [.cla-signatures.md](.cla-signatures.md) and
commits it before merging. **That file is the record of agreement**, not the
comment: a comment can be edited or deleted by its author, while a committed row
is timestamped by someone else and travels with the code.
