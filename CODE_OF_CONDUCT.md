# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, never about the person.

## What that means here

**Expected:**

- Take the other person's point seriously before disagreeing with it.
- Give feedback on code, not on the author.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a proposal, and that a maintainer
  saying no to a feature is not a slight.
- Remember that most contributors are doing this in their own time.

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including about someone's background,
  identity, disability or beliefs.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Persistently derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other project
space, and to public spaces when you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **aneeshbhat@devvoid.org**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential.

Because this is a small project, there is no committee and no appeals process.
The maintainer will look at what happened, talk to the people involved where
that helps, and act: usually a warning, sometimes a block. That is the whole
procedure, and pretending otherwise would be theatre.
